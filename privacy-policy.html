Privacy Policy

Purchase Protection Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026
Application: Purchase Protection (Package ID: com.solodevnikhil.purchaseprotection)
Developer: Solodev-Nikhil

Core Privacy Guarantee: Purchase Protection is a local-first Android utility. The app operates without user accounts, cloud databases, or remote logins. Your purchase records, receipt photos, and warranty dates reside solely in your device's private storage.

1. Overview & Architecture

This Privacy Policy explains how Purchase Protection ("the app"), created and published by Solodev-Nikhil, handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to transparency and respecting your digital privacy.

  • No User Accounts: You do not need to register, log in, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use Purchase Protection.
  • No Cloud Backend: The application does not maintain proprietary cloud databases, external user profiles, or remote synchronization servers.
  • No Data Scraping or AI/OCR: The app does not scrape user emails, connect to financial institutions, or process receipts through external AI, machine learning, or OCR services.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

All data entered into Purchase Protection is stored strictly within the app's sandboxed local SQLite database (powered by Android Room architecture) and local internal storage:

  • Purchase Details: Item names, store/merchant names, purchase prices, currency choices, purchase categories, purchase dates, return window deadlines, warranty expiration dates, and personal notes.
  • Receipt Images: Photos or documents you choose to attach to a purchase record. These images are imported via the secure Android system Photo Picker and saved exclusively to the app's private internal storage directory. They are never uploaded to any remote server.
  • Reminder Configurations: Local alarm triggers and scheduled notifications for return deadlines and warranty expirations.

3. Advertising & Privacy Choices (Free Tier)

Purchase Protection is offered as a free application supported by non-intrusive mobile advertising for Free users. We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob):

  • Ad Formats: Eligible Free users may see banner ads and an interstitial ad displayed after saving a purchase.
  • Data Processed by Google AdMob: To serve, measure, and protect against fraud, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process device identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID / AD_ID), IP address (used to infer coarse location/country for localized ads), network connection information, and ad interaction telemetry.
  • Consent Management (Google UMP): In jurisdictions requiring user consent (such as the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland under GDPR/ePrivacy, as well as applicable US state privacy laws), the app displays Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog.
  • Changing Your Privacy Choices: You can review, update, or withdraw your advertising and consent choices at any time directly within the app by navigating to Settings → Privacy Choices.
  • Pro Tier Ad-Free Guarantee: Users who upgrade to Purchase Protection Pro enjoy a completely ad-free experience. When Pro is active, the app completely deactivates ad units, and no ad requests are transmitted to Google Mobile Ads.

4. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The app offers an optional one-time lifetime upgrade called Purchase Protection Pro:

  • All purchases and transactions are processed directly by Google Play via the Google Play Billing Library.
  • Purchase Protection does not collect, process, or store credit card numbers, debit card details, billing addresses, or banking information.
  • Google Play provides the app with an anonymous purchase token to verify your lifetime entitlement status.

5. Android Device Permissions

Purchase Protection requests only permissions that are strictly necessary to deliver its core tracking and reminder functionality:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google Play Billing license verification and for Google Mobile Ads / UMP consent requests on the Free tier.
  • android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Required on Android 13+ to deliver local return deadline and warranty expiration reminder notifications.
  • android.permission.SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM: Allows the app to schedule precise reminders for return windows and warranty expirations so alerts fire at the exact date and time you set.
  • android.permission.RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED: Used solely to restore and re-register your scheduled local reminders if your device restarts.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Required by Google Play Billing to enable the Pro lifetime upgrade.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google Play Billing to check network connectivity before initiating network calls.
  • Photo Selection: The app utilizes the modern Android Photo Picker. This allows you to select receipt images without granting the app broad read access to your device's photo library or external storage.

6. Analytics & Crash Reporting

Purchase Protection does not include third-party behavioral analytics tools, session recording software, or proprietary crash SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, or Facebook SDK). General, non-identifying aggregate technical diagnostics (such as crash stack traces and ANRs) may be collected automatically by Google Play according to your Android device system settings.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all application data is stored locally on your device, you retain full autonomy over your records:

  • Individual Item Deletion: You can delete any individual purchase or receipt photo at any time from within the purchase details screen.
  • Bulk Deletion: You can wipe all purchases, receipts, and local records at once using the Settings → Clear All Purchases option inside the app.
  • App Uninstallation: Uninstalling Purchase Protection from your Android device immediately and permanently deletes the local SQLite database, cached files, and all stored receipt images from your device.

8. Children's Privacy

Purchase Protection is a general consumer utility designed for tracking purchases and warranties. It is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or under the age of 16 in applicable European jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children.

9. Security

Because your data is saved locally on your device, its security is protected by Android's application sandboxing architecture. No unauthorized third party or remote server can access your purchase records or receipts from the app. We recommend using device-level security (PIN, password, or biometrics) to safeguard your device.

10. Third-Party Services

The app relies on trusted third-party services provided by Google LLC for specific functionality:

  • Google Mobile Ads / AdMob: Google Advertising Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services & Billing: Google Privacy Policy
