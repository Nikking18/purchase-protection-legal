Purchase Protection

A private, on-device utility to track return windows, organize receipts, and manage warranty expiration deadlines.

Developer: Solodev-Nikhil

Platform: Android (Google Play)

About the Application

Purchase Protection is engineered with a strict local-first philosophy. Your purchase history, receipt images, price records, and warranty dates reside directly in your device's secure internal database (Room), never on remote application servers.

🔒 100% Local Storage
No cloud servers, no account registration, and no login required. Your data belongs entirely to you.
🔔 Timely Reminders
Local notifications alert you before return windows close or manufacturer warranties expire.
📸 On-Device Receipts
Attach receipt photos via the secure Android photo picker; images are stored strictly in private app storage.
⭐ Optional Pro Upgrade
A one-time lifetime purchase via Google Play Billing unlocks an ad-free experience and unlimited tracking.
